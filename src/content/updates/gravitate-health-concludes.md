The Gravitate-Health project, which began in November 2020, came to an end on 30 June 2026. Gravitate has been set up to carry its mission forward.

## What Gravitate-Health set out to do

Gravitate-Health was a European public–private partnership supported by the Innovative Medicines Initiative (IMI2, grant agreement 945334). According to the project's public record, its mission was to "equip and empower citizens with digital information tools" that help people use medicines safely and achieve better health outcomes.

Its central idea was the **Gravitate Lens (G-lens)**: a way of presenting the approved information about a medicine so that what matters most to each person comes into focus, without concealing or filtering anything. The project worked with the HL7 FHIR standard for electronic product information (ePI), developed open-source components, and built a shared set of user personas so that design decisions stayed grounded in the needs of real people.

## Why continue

A research project has a start and an end date. The problem it worked on does not. People still struggle to find and understand trustworthy information about their medicines, and digital product information is still being put into practice across Europe.

Gravitate exists to keep that work going as an ongoing, collaborative effort for the public good, open to patient organisations, health professionals, researchers, industry, standards bodies and public institutions.

## What this means in practice

Gravitate is at an early stage. Over the coming months we will be talking with organisations and communities about shared priorities across our [four areas of focus](/our-focus/). Taking part in Gravitate-Health does not automatically make an organisation a partner of Gravitate: any partnerships will be agreed and announced openly.

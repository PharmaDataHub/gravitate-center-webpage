Start with what the opportunity is and who it is for, in one or two sentences.

## What is involved

Describe the time commitment, format and any support offered (for example, expenses or accessibility support).

## How to express interest

Explain the next step and the deadline. Link to the [Collaborate page](/collaborate/) if people should get in touch.
